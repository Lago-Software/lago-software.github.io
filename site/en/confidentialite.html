Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you visit this website or write to us, why, and how to exercise your rights, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act (loi Informatique et Libertés). The French version of this policy prevails.

Data controller

Lago, trade name of Hugo Lehéricé, sole trader (entrepreneur individuel). Contact: lago.software.contact@gmail.com.

Data processed

Demo request. When you use the contact form, your email app opens with a pre-filled message: name, venue, email address, type of business and your message. The website itself stores nothing; we only receive the message you choose to send.

Purpose: to answer your request and present Lago. Legal basis: pre-contractual measures taken at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Retention: no more than three years after our last exchange, then deletion.

Host’s technical logs. Like any web server, the host GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States and states that it adheres to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in having a secure website.

Cookies and trackers

This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers, analytics tools, advertising pixels or embedded third-party content. Fonts are hosted on the website itself. No consent banner is therefore needed.

Recipients

Your information is reserved for the publisher of Lago. It is never sold, rented or transferred to third parties.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. To exercise these rights, write to us at the address above. If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr), or with the authority in your country.

Data entered in the Lago software

The data that businesses record in the Lago app (customers, bookings, sales, schedules) is processed on their behalf, under their terms of use. This policy only covers this presentation website.
