Frequently asked questions

All about Lago

Is Lago cash register software?

+

Yes. Lago is touchscreen cash register software that meets French cash register rules and runs on a tablet, computer or phone. It is also online booking, time slot ticketing, staff scheduling and safety check software: everything is in the same app.

Is Lago suitable for a trampoline park, laser tag arena or escape room?

+

Yes. Lago was designed in a trampoline park and adapts to leisure venues that sell time slots: laser tag, bowling, escape rooms, go-karting, indoor play centres, climbing gyms, treetop adventure parks. It also suits local shops: hair salons, beauty salons, restaurants and retail shops.

What is Lago?

+

Lago is POS and management software designed for leisure venues (trampoline parks, laser tag, bowling alleys, indoor play centres) and local shops (hair salons, restaurants, retail shops). It brings together the touchscreen POS, customer and staff scheduling, online booking, loyalty and safety checks in a single app.

Which devices does the Lago POS run on?

+

Lago runs in the browser or installs like an app on tablets, computers and smartphones. Data is synced across all the team’s devices and updates are automatic.

Does Lago comply with French cash register software rules?

+

Lago is designed to meet the data inalterability, security, retention and archiving requirements of Article 286 of the French General Tax Code: numbered receipts, sealed daily, monthly and annual closures, an event log and exportable archives. Since the 2026 French Finance Act, the publisher’s individual certificate is accepted again: Lago provides it using the tax authority’s official template.

Can I take bookings online?

+

Yes. Lago provides a booking page to embed on your website: customers choose an available time slot and the request goes straight into your schedule. You can also offer online payment, in full or as a deposit, through your own Stripe account: the money goes straight to you, with no Lago fees.

Is Lago suitable for a hair salon, restaurant or retail shop?

+

Yes. The item and service catalogue, checkout, customer records, loyalty, stock and statistics adapt to your business, and the administrator chooses which modules the team can see.

Do I have to print a receipt for every sale?

+

Not in France. Since 1 August 2023, receipts are no longer printed automatically: they are given when the customer asks. With Lago, you print the receipt on request and can find a duplicate at any time.

Is my data protected?

+

Every account is approved by the administrator, who decides what each person can see and change. Data is synced in an online database and Lago offers automatic backups.

How much does Lago cost?

+

Lago Essential costs €29 excl. VAT per month and Lago Complete, POS included, €59 excl. VAT per month, per location. Unlimited users, updates included, no commitment, and the first month is free. No Lago commission on online payments: only Stripe fees apply.
