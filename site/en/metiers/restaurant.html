Frequently asked questions

Restaurants: your questions

Does Lago handle the floor plan and sending orders to the kitchen?

+

Not yet. Today Lago focuses on checkout, reservations, loyalty, the team and compliance. Tell us during the demo if these features are essential for you.

Can I have several VAT rates?

+

Yes. Each item has its own rate, and the ticket breaks VAT down by rate.

Can I split the bill?

+

Yes. A single ticket can be paid in several instalments and with several payment methods, for example partly by card and partly in cash.
