Frequently asked questions

Trampoline parks: your questions

Can I limit the number of jumpers per session?

+

Yes. You enter your park’s capacity: each 15-minute slot shows the number present and changes colour as it fills up. Items marked “counts as jumper” are included in the capacity.

Are coloured wristbands supported?

+

Yes. Lago assigns a wristband colour based on the start time and length of the session, and shows it at the till when the sale is made.

How do I handle birthday parties?

+

Create an event with its room, times and number of guests: it appears in the customer schedule, and you can prepare a quote for the family.

Does the daily check replace the paper log?

+

The visual check is done on a tablet or phone before opening, and each completed check produces a time-stamped PDF filed in the log. Check with your insurer or inspection body which format they expect.
