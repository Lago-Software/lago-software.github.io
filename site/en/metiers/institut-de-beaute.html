What changes with Lago

Packages and gift cards without paper cards

A package of five treatments becomes a session pass: it is created at the sale, with its code, and counted down at each visit. Gift cards are sold and redeemed directly at the till.

  • Session passes
  • Gift cards
  • Promo codes with dates and conditions

Real Lago screenshot · demo data

Loyalty that brings your customers back

€1 spent = 1 point. At each tier, the customer gets a discount on her next visit, and an automatic email lets her know if she has opted in.

  • Customisable tiers
  • Email when a tier is reached
  • Ranking of top customers

Treatments and products on the same ticket

Each item has its own VAT rate, and the ticket itemises them. Closures are sealed and the publisher’s certificate is provided on the official template.

  • VAT rate on each item
  • Several payment methods on one ticket
  • Compliant daily closures

Frequently asked questions

Beauty salons: your questions

Does Lago offer an appointment diary for each beautician?

+

Not in that form yet. Lago manages time slots and bookings, but not yet an individual diary per practitioner. Tell us during the demo if it is essential for you.

Can I sell treatment packages?

+

Yes, with session passes: the package is created at the sale and counted down at each visit.

Is Lago compliant for selling to consumers in France?

+

Lago is designed to meet the French requirements for inalterability, security, retention and archiving, and provides the publisher’s certificate based on the official template.
