Frequently asked questions

Pricing and getting started

How much does Lago cost?

+

Lago Essential costs €29 excl. VAT per month and Lago Complete, POS included, €59 excl. VAT per month, per location. Unlimited users, updates included, no commitment, and the first month is free. No Lago commission on online payments: only Stripe fees apply.

Is the demo paid?

+

No, it is free and comes with no obligation.

Do I need to buy hardware?

+

No. Lago runs on the tablet, computer or phone you already have, with your usual payment terminal.

Are there fees on online payments?

+

Lago takes no commission: online payments go through your own Stripe account, and only Stripe’s fees apply.

Can I start with just a few modules?

+

Yes. The Essential plan covers scheduling, the team and checks; you can add the POS and online booking later.

Is there a minimum commitment?

+

No. The monthly subscription has no commitment: you can stop at the end of any month. Paying annually gives you two months free.

What happens after the free month?

+

You choose whether to continue. Nothing is charged without your consent, and your data remains exportable.
