Why Lago

Born on the floor, for people on the floor

Lago was conceived in a trampoline park, from a simple observation: a till on one side, a paper diary on the other, the team schedule in a spreadsheet and the checks in a binder. We wanted to bring it all together, without making anything more complicated.
