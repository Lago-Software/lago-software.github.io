Online booking and payment, linked to your schedule

Lago publishes your available places on a booking page to embed in your website. The customer chooses a slot and can pay in full or a deposit on a page in your colours. The money goes straight to your Stripe account, and the amount already paid is deducted when they pay the balance at reception.

  • Available places in real time, at the till and online
  • Deposit as a percentage or per participant
  • No Lago fees: only Stripe fees apply

Frequently asked questions

Your questions

Is online payment available?

+

Yes. You can ask for the full amount or a deposit, as a percentage or per participant, on a payment page in your colours. Payment goes through your own Stripe account: the money goes straight to you, Lago sees no bank data and takes no fees. The team checks the payment in one click, and the amount already paid is pre-filled at checkout.

Are online and counter bookings mixed together?

+

They arrive in the same schedule, which avoids double bookings: each slot’s capacity takes both into account.

Can I manage private events?

+

Yes. An event reserves a room and a time, and you can prepare a quote for the customer.
