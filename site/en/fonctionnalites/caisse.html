Frequently asked questions

Your questions

What hardware does the POS run on?

+

On a tablet, computer or smartphone, in the browser or as an installed app. Card payments are taken on your usual payment terminal.

Do I have to print a receipt for every sale?

+

Not in France. Since 1 August 2023, the receipt is only given if the customer asks for it, except in the cases set out by law. Lago lets you print the receipt on request and find its duplicate.

Does Lago comply with the French anti-fraud law?

+

Lago is designed to meet the four conditions of Article 286 of the French General Tax Code and provides the publisher’s certificate, accepted again since the 2026 French Finance Act.
